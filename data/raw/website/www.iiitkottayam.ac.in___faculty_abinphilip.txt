SHERLOCK, Digital Forensics and Offensive Security Lab at IIIT Kottayam
Faculty In Charge for SHERLOCK labs, was responsible for setting up the Digital Forensic Laboratory as part of academic, research and training requirement at IIIT Kottayam
The lab is equipped with the hardware and software to perform digital forensic investigations in a scientific manner.
The lab is equipped with FRED Forensic High End Workstation, DI Ultrakit V5 and DI Ultrablock kits for data acquisition, Magnet Axiom Cyber Academic License, Major Open Source Digital forensic Software’s, High End Machines and Mobile devices.
The lab Hosts Industrial Visits as part of FDPs and Visits as part of Social Outreach Program for school and college students.
The lab facility is utilised in the conduct of training programs (cybercommands, Kerala Police cyber operations), FDPs, workshops and internships in the domain of Digital Forensics.
Research Activities at the laboratory involve research in IoT Forensics, Ai for Digital Forensics, Building of datasets for Digital Forensics, Memory Forensics and Mobile Apk Vulnerability Analysis.
