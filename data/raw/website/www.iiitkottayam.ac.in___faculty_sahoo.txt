Background
Jayakrushna Sahoo received his Ph.D. with specialization in Data Mining from Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur, India and obtained his M.Tech. degree in Computer Science and Engineering from International Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Bhubaneswar, India. He has been associated with IIIT Kottayam since July 2019. Prior to this, he has experience of working with BML Munjal University, Gurgaon, India, in the capacity of Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering. Dr. Sahoo has also worked as Ad Hoc faculty in the
Department of Computer Applications, National Institute of Technology (NIT), Jamshedpur, India. His current research interests include data mining, Machine Learning and cryptography.
