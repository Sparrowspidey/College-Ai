Scholarship
Note:
Please be informed that no direct financial assistance is available from the Institute. After admission to the Institute, students can apply for any scholarships, if they wish, directly to the respective State Government or other authorities of their choice. The Institute actively supports such applications by providing all necessary documents and forwarding them to the appropriate authorities to facilitate the timely processing of benefits for students.
