Research/Academic Partnerships and International Collaborations
My research initiatives are greatly enhanced through numerous partnerships and international collaborations. One of the key collaborations is the UK India Future Networks Initiative (UKI-FNI), funded by the EPSRC, United Kingdom, in 2022, in partnership with the University of Glasgow, UK. This initiative focuses on developing advanced network technologies to improve communication infrastructures between the UK and India. Additionally, in 2024, I secured the UKIERI-MeitY Grant, also in collaboration with the University of Glasgow, which strengthens UK-India collaborations in emerging technologies such as cyber-physical systems and IoT.
Significant collaborations include the DST-DAAD Grant awarded in 2023 and the IGSTC (DST) – SING Grant awarded in 2024, both in partnership with the University of Offenburg, Germany. These projects focus on advanced antenna technologies for 5G networks and sustainable, innovative green technologies. Furthermore, I am collaborating with Universiti Teknologi Brunei and Ajou University, South Korea, on AI for climate resilience. Similar efforts are ongoing with the University of Cambodia and the Asian Institute of Technology, Thailand, focusing on IoT and HPC solutions. In partnership with the Toyota Foundation, Japan, we are addressing sea-level rise and climate influence. Additionally, our work with the University of Agder, Norway, and National Cheng Chung University, Taiwan, focuses on AI and cybersecurity research.
