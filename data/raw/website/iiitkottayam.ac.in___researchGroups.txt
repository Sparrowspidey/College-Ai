The campus is equipped with an internet bandwidth of 5 Gbps primary and 1 Gbps secondary lines (1:1). Internet connectivity is also extended to the boys' and girls' hostels through both wired and wireless connections. More than 250 access points are deployed throughout the campus to provide these services. LAN connectivity is also extended to all hostels on the campus. There are no restrictions on timing or limits for internet usage. All academic premises are covered with WiFi. The main buildings are connected with a 10 Gbps LAN backbone using multimode fiber media.
At present, there are 5 computer labs with 500 high-end systems and 20 workstations, all equipped with wired connectivity. More than 2,800 users (including staff and students) are connected to the institute network through proper authentication methods. Around 200 IP phones are installed at various locations inside and outside the campus to ensure easy communication. A 24x7 CCTV surveillance system is also operational on the campus for added security.
