Biography :
Dr. G. Kumaresan is an academician with 3 years and 5 months of post-PhD teaching experience and 5 years and 6 months of pre-PhD research experience. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Applications from the National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli, and is a qualified postgraduate with M.Tech, MCA, and M.Phil. He is a member of IAENG, IET, and ISOC. His broad research interests include Cellular Automata in the Context of Cryptography, Artificial Intelligence, and Quantum Computing. He is a recipient of the UGC National Doctoral Fellowship (2015–2020) and the UGC National Post Graduate Scholarship (2011–2013)./
